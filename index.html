1Kişisel veri toplanmıyor

Star Isles adını, e-postanı, konumunu, rehberini ya da cihaz kimliğini istemez ve kaydetmez. Hesap açman gerekmez.

Oyunun yayınlanan sürümünde internet izni yoktur. Teknik olarak hiçbir veri gönderemez ya da alamaz; oyun tamamen çevrimdışı oynanır.

Reklam ağı, kullanım istatistiği toplayan bir araç veya üçüncü taraf bir hizmet kullanılmaz. Uygulama içi satın alma da yoktur.

Privacy Policy (English)

Star Isles collects, stores and shares no personal data. The game runs fully offline: the published build declares no internet permission, so it is technically unable to send or receive any data.

1. No personal data

The game never asks for or records your name, e-mail, location, contacts or device identifiers. No account is required.

2. Everything stays on your device

Your save file, settings and high-score table are kept only in your phone's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Neither we nor any third party can access them.

3. No network access

The published build has no internet permission and therefore cannot transmit anything. The game is played entirely offline.

4. No ads, no analytics

No advertising network, no usage analytics, no third-party services. No in-app purchases either.

5. Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its "last updated" date will be updated before a new version of the app is released.
